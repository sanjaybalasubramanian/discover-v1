================================================================================
🔍 WHY GMAIL BLOCKS TRACKING PIXELS
================================================================================

Gmail has MULTIPLE layers of blocking:

1. GMAIL IMAGE PROXY
   - Gmail doesn't load images directly from your server
   - Instead: Gmail proxy → Checks domain → Caches image → Shows to user
   - Blocks suspicious domains like loca.lt, ngrok-free.app
   
2. TUNNELING DOMAIN BLOCKLIST
   Gmail blocks known tunneling services:
   ❌ loca.lt (LocalTunnel)
   ❌ ngrok-free.app (free ngrok)
   ❌ localhost.run
   ❌ serveo.net
   
   Why? These are used for phishing, malware, spam
   
3. UNTRUSTED/NEW DOMAINS
   Even legitimate domains get blocked if:
   - Domain too new (< 30 days)
   - No HTTPS/SSL
   - Suspicious TLD (.tk, .ml, .ga)
   
   Gmail trusts:
   ✅ mailchimp.com, sendgrid.net, amazonaws.com
   ✅ Your custom domain (if established)

4. USER IMAGE SETTINGS
   - "Ask before displaying" (default for unknown senders)
   - Gmail shows: "Images are not displayed"
